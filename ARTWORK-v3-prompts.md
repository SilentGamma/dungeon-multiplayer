# Minimal token revision (v3)

Built-in image-generation tool. Edit targets: corresponding v2 tokens; style reference: unchanged Joker v2 token. Selection portraits retain v2 shading and designs.

Final assets: 128×128 transparent lossless WebP under server/public.

## brawler

Saved: server/public/brawler-token-v3.webp

Use case: style-transfer. Edit target image 1 (brawler token), style reference image 2 (Joker token). Redesign the brawler in-game circular face token to match Joker's extreme minimalism, flat two-color silhouette shapes, smooth bold curves, huge readable eyes and eyebrows, sparse expressive features. Keep the brawler's role color and identity: coral female fighter, fierce eyebrows, short chunky side-swept bangs; reduce hair to 3 large shapes, no individual strands, no detailed anime eyes. Match Joker's line weight, minimal eye marks, flat single light role-color fill and very dark ink. Single circular badge filling square canvas, same scale and framing as Joker. No shading, no lighting, no gradients, no hatching, no texture, no text. Transparent outside circular badge. At most 8-10 facial shapes total. This must look like a coherent icon family, readable at 32 pixels.
